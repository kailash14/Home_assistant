# Exterview AI — Product Strategy FY26

## Mission
Help engineering and product hiring teams make better decisions in less time, using AI to remove low-signal work from the interview loop.

## Strategic Pillars (FY26)

### Pillar 1: Signal over volume
Reduce time-to-hire by surfacing the highest-signal candidates earlier. We compete on *quality* of the interview loop, not quantity of resumes processed.

### Pillar 2: Interviewer enablement
Make every interviewer better. The product is a coach for the hiring manager and panel, not a replacement for them. Human judgment stays in the loop.

### Pillar 3: Defensible AI
AI features must be auditable, bias-checked, and compliant with EU AI Act + emerging US state laws (NYC AEDT, IL AI Video Interview Act). Compliance is a moat, not a tax.

### Pillar 4: Enterprise-grade platform
Cosmos DB + Durable Functions backbone. SOC 2 Type II achieved. ISO 42001 in flight. Every feature ships with audit logs, RBAC, and retention controls.

## Out of scope for FY26

- Sourcing / outbound candidate discovery (we are interview-stage, not top-of-funnel)
- ATS replacement (we integrate with Greenhouse/Lever/Workday; we do not replace them)
- Free-tier or PLG motion (enterprise sales-led only)
- Consumer-facing candidate coaching products

## OKRs (snapshot)

- O1: Ship 3 audit-grade AI features (each with bias eval, audit log, opt-out)
- O2: Reach $5M ARR with ≥80% net retention
- O3: 10 named enterprise logos with multi-team deployment
